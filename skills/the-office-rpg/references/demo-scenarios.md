## 💰 The budget ask
**Content:** "Hi team, wanted to share some thoughts on a partner hackathon in Q3. Rough budget $40K. Let me know what you think!"
**Goal:** approval for a $40K partner hackathon.
**Recommended cast:** 🚀 Priya (approves the budget), 🧭 Marc (his team does the work), 📎 Michael (wildcard, for fun).
**Why it works as a first try:** no clear ask, no metric, no owner, so the fixes are obvious and the meter moves.
